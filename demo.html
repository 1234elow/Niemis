📋 Available Endpoints

  • /api/auth - Authentication (login, register)
  • /api/schools - School management
  • /api/students - Student profiles and SPIS data
  • /api/teachers - Staff management
  • /api/attendance - Attendance tracking
  • /api/facilities - Facility management
  • /api/reports - Reports and analytics
  • /api/rfid - RFID device management
